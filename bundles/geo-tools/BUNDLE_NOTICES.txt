Mere Geo Tools bundle acknowledgments

Rasterio's macOS wheel includes GDAL, PROJ, OpenSSL, cURL, NetCDF, HDF5,
and other native libraries. The exact wheel dylib inventory, upstream source
archives, versions, checksums, and license-text mappings are preserved in
geo-native-inventory.json and geo-native-notices.txt. Rasterio's BSD license is
also preserved in dependency-licenses. GDAL's consolidated end-user license
and notices remain available in the frozen application's
rasterio/gdal_data/LICENSE.TXT.

Numcodecs compiles bundled c-blosc, LZ4, zlib, and Zstandard sources into its
macOS extensions. Safetensors includes a Rust extension. Their reviewed source
archives, Cargo lock, crate checksums, conservative non-development Cargo
dependency closure, and license texts are recorded in the same Geo native
inventory and notice files. The Cargo inventory includes build and target
dependencies and can exceed the linked macOS runtime set.

This software is based in part on the work of the Independent JPEG Group.
Portions of this software are copyright The FreeType Project (www.freetype.org).
All rights reserved. Pillow's full license file, including its native library
notices, is preserved in dependency-licenses.

The reviewed asciitree 0.3.3 source archive is fetched by immutable URL and
SHA-256, built with the pinned builder toolchain, and its MIT license is
preserved in dependency-licenses. All other Python runtime dependencies are
installed from hash-locked binary wheels only. Their supplied license files are
preserved in dependency-licenses. CPython and its native dependency notices are
preserved in python-licenses. The plugin and support package MIT terms are
preserved in LICENSE.txt and dependency-licenses.

The PyInstaller bootloader is used without modification under its distribution
exception. Its licensing terms, including the Apache-licensed runtime hooks,
are preserved in dependency-licenses.

No Earth-observation source data, model weights, AWS credentials, requester-pays
credentials, or STAC credentials are redistributed in this bundle. OlmoEarth
model artifacts and their use restrictions remain an upstream core-runtime
concern. User-initiated requester-pays access may incur provider charges.

License expressions in the build inventory do not replace the upstream terms.
