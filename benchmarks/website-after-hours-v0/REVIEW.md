# Review the websites

For reviewers, this guide separates how a site works from how it looks and feels.
Record an entry letter, stage, reviewer ID, date, and evidence paths with each
rating. Hide model names, timings, and token counts until ratings are complete.

Use a scale from one to five: **1** means unusable or absent; **2** means major
problems; **3** means adequate with visible issues; **4** means strong with minor
issues; **5** means consistently clear and polished. Leave unreviewed ratings
blank. Add a concrete observation for each rating.

| Dimension | Review |
| --- | --- |
| Visual hierarchy | Can you distinguish the primary task, room details, and secondary actions? |
| Typography and spacing | Are text sizes, line lengths, rhythm, and alignment readable and consistent? |
| Visual identity | Do color, imagery, and components form a coherent identity appropriate to a creative studio? |
| Responsive composition | Do both required viewports feel deliberately arranged and complete? |
| Booking usability | Can you find a suitable room, understand availability, see the full price, and recover from an error? |
| Accessibility | Can you finish with a keyboard, identify focus, understand labels and errors, and read content at 200% zoom? |

Capture the browse page, a room detail, a booking confirmation, and an empty or
invalid state at both viewports. Use the same room and date when possible.
Review hover, focus, and error states in the working site, not only screenshots.

For the timed usability task, start with empty visitor storage. Find a room for
four people with microphones, costing at most CAD 70 per hour. Book October 13,
2026, from 14:00 to 15:00, refresh, then cancel it. Record completion, seconds,
wrong turns, and assistance. Give no assistance before marking an unassisted
attempt unsuccessful. Repeat stage 2 with a reviewer who has not learned that
site where possible; otherwise disclose the practice effect.

For accessibility, inspect form names, keyboard order, focus visibility, dialog
behavior when used, error announcements, contrast, and 200% zoom. Record the
method and any automatic scanner findings separately. This review is not an
accessibility conformance certification.

For pairwise preference, show two anonymous sites and ask which better supports
the booking task. Allow a tie and require a short reason. Retain the six
dimension ratings alongside preferences; do not turn taste into functional
correctness.
